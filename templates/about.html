About

Hi 👋 my names Joe and I work in the conservation sector. Welcome to A plant a day, this is a blog I'm using to document my progress in working towards my FISC test. What is a FISC? It stands for Field Identification Skills Certificate and is a test devised by BSBI in order to determine botanical proficiency - more info can be seen here

I decided to make this blog in order to encourage me to spend a bit of time each day recording and looking at a single plant species.
